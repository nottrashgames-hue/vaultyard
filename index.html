Damage sphere
The floating ball of flesh past the bench: skin, fat and muscle over a bone core. Shots carve an entry, a channel and an exit; wounds bleed down the skin, drip, and bruise over the next minutes. Blood stays where it lands.

Shotgun (second bench)

Other hand
Grip the forend to aim two-handed; pull it back and push it forward to pump
Load
Bring a shell up to the underside of the gun anywhere under the receiver or tube and it goes in (6 in the tube). The pouch gives shells while you hold it.
Close range

Reset

Ragdoll NPC (down the shotgun lane)

Physics
Fully simulated body with muscle motors and balance. Shove it and it stumbles, stepping to catch itself; push hard enough and it falls, then gets back up after a moment. Grab a limb and drag it
Damage
Skin, muscle, bone and organs in every part. A brain or heart hit is fatal (the heart takes a few seconds); heavy bleeding kills too; leg hits make it buckle
Blood
The tag over his head shows his blood volume. He goes paler as he loses it, presses a hand on his worst wound (which slows it), passes out below 60% and dies at 45%
Dismemberment
Shoot a limb or the body clean through and the two parts separate. He can live through losing an arm or a leg; not a torso split or his head
Machete
On the pistol bench. Chop and the edge cuts into the flesh until it slows to a stop and sticks; pull it back out, or drive it on harder to keep cutting. Bone stops it. Cut through a limb and it comes off
Organs
Open the belly wide enough (shotgun, or several hits close together) and the intestines spill out. You can grab them
Ammo pouch
On your left hip. Reach in with your free hand and grip to pull a magazine for the gun you're holding. Let a magazine go inside the pouch to put it away.
